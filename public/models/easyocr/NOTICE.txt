EasyOCR models (detector.int8.onnx, recognizer.int8.onnx)

Source:   Qualcomm AI Hub Models, EasyOCR, release v0.62.2, ONNX w8a8 export
          https://huggingface.co/qualcomm/EasyOCR
          https://aihub.qualcomm.com/models/easyocr
Upstream: EasyOCR by JaidedAI, https://github.com/JaidedAI/EasyOCR
License:  Apache License 2.0

Modification: the published export stores float32 weights and quantizes them
inside the graph. scripts/fold-aihub-weights.py precomputes those constant
QuantizeLinear nodes and stores the INT8 result, which reduces the files from
98 MB to 25 MB. Model outputs are bit-identical to the published export.
